Training programs

Every dog and every household is different, so sessions are built around what you're dealing with day to day rather than a fixed script.

Puppies

Puppy foundations

Name recognition, handling, crate settling, house-training routines, and the early groundwork that makes the next year easier for both of you.

All ages

Everyday obedience

Sit, stay, come, and loose-leash walking, practiced in the places you actually use them — the front door, the sidewalk, the car.

Adult dogs

Behavior support

Work on jumping, reactivity on leash, resource guarding, or over-excitement with visitors. We start with what triggers it and build a plan from there.

1-on-1

Private coaching sessions

Focused time with your trainer, at your home or a nearby quiet location, so you can ask questions and practice in real time.

Common questions

A few things people usually ask before their first session.

How long does training usually take?+
It depends on the dog and what you're working on. Basic manners often show progress within a handful of sessions; longer-standing behavior issues can take more time. We'll give you a realistic estimate after meeting your dog rather than a fixed number up front.
Do you work with reactive or nervous dogs?+
Yes. We start at whatever distance or setting your dog is comfortable with and work forward from there at their pace. If a case needs veterinary or behavioral specialist involvement, we'll say so directly.
Where do sessions happen?+
Most sessions take place at your home, since that's where the behaviors show up. Some skills are practiced at a quiet nearby location once your dog is ready for it.
How do I book a session?+
Call (559) 222-6229 or send a message through the contact form below. We'll ask a few questions about your dog and get you on the schedule.
What areas do you serve?+
Fresno, CA and the surrounding area. If you're outside that range, call and ask — we can let you know if a visit is workable.
